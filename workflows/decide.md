# Decide

**Input:** problem model and alternatives.

**Do:** compare viable approaches against behavior, complexity, security, cost, operability, and rollback. Name non-goals and the assumption most worth testing.

**Output:** a human-approved direction, rejected alternatives, and safe-stop conditions.

**Gate:** the agent may frame the choice but must not silently decide a consequential tradeoff.

**Evidence:** decision note, approved issue, experiment, or test that supports the choice.
